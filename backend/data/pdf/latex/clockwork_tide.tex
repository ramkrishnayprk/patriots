\section*{Synopsis}
Apprentice horologist Elian Voss lives in a harbor where the tides arrive by
bell schedule. When the sea stops retreating, she discovers that the ancient
clock beneath the lighthouse has lost one of its silver teeth. The missing
piece was carried beyond the mapped ocean by the disgraced Captain Brann.

Elian joins Brann's small crew aboard a sail-powered workshop. Their voyage
crosses forests of floating kelp, islands that surface for only an hour, and a
storm that repeats the same minute. At the edge of the world they meet the
Keeper, who reveals that the tide machine is failing because generations have
forced it to hold the sea in an unnatural rhythm. Repairing it will save the
harbor but flood the kingdom's abandoned coastal walls.

\section*{Themes and Style}
The adventure combines maritime fantasy with intricate mechanical imagery.
Its central questions concern inherited responsibility, adaptation, and the
danger of treating nature as a machine that can be permanently controlled.
Humor comes from the crew's improvised inventions, while the emotional story
follows Elian learning to challenge the rules she was trained to preserve.
